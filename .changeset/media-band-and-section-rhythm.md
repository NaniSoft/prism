Add `MediaBand01`, an editorial band with a caller-owned media slot, and a `size`
prop on `Section` for compact, default and tall vertical rhythms.

The band owns the frame, the ratio, the surface and the order, and it never puts
the copy on top of an arbitrary photograph. The media is the caller's node, so an
image, a video poster or a composed figure can sit in the frame. The Section size
keeps the existing default byte-identical and gives a page three authored rhythms
instead of five site stylesheets inventing their own.
