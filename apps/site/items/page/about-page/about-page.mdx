## Overview

AboutPage is a complete about screen. It composes five bands in one fixed order
and passes each one its own content, so the page adds the sequence and nothing
else: the statement band, the milestone rail, the team grid, the engagement grid
and a closing way on.

| Order | Band | Question it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | About01 | What is this, and for whom? |
| 2 | Story01 | How did it get here? |
| 3 | Team01 | Who is behind it? |
| 4 | Services01 | What does it do? |
| 5 | the caller's closing band | How do I reach it? |

**The order is the decision the page makes.** Each of the five is a question a
reader arrives with, and this is the order they are asked in: a reader does not
want the history before they know what the thing is, and a name with no history
behind it is a logo.

**The order costs something.** A company whose story belongs after the team
cannot reorder this page. The honest answer is to compose two pages and accept
two page rhythms where the author wanted one, which is the correct price of an
opinionated order. An `order` prop would have moved the decision to every call
site, which is to say it would have stopped being a page.

**The page does not decide which bands appear, only in what order.** `milestones`,
`people`, `services` and `contact` are all optional, and omitting one removes it
without moving any other, so a product with no story to tell passes no
`milestones` and reads the same four questions in the same order.

## Usage

```tsx
import { AboutPage } from '@nanisoft/prism-ui/pages/about-page'

<AboutPage
  title="We run the thing we sell"
  description="One or two sentences under the heading."
  statement="A large sentence saying what this is, in the present tense."
  principles={principles}
  milestones={milestones}
  people={people}
  services={engagements}
  contact={{
    title: 'A closing line',
    description: 'A sentence that says what happens next.',
    cta: { label: 'Get in touch', href: '/contact' },
  }}
/>
```

## Guidelines

### When to use

Use AboutPage for a public about route whose job is to answer, in this order,
what a company is, how it got here, who is behind it and what it does.

Use it with a subset. A product with no history to publish passes no `milestones`
and the remaining four questions still arrive in the same order.

### When not to use

Do not use it for a product page. A product page answers what the thing does and
what it costs, and `MarketingPage` is the composition for that.

Do not use it for a company whose people are the argument and whose story belongs
after them. Compose two pages, one holding the team and one holding the history.

Do not compose `Contact01` into the closing band. Its `onSubmit` is a function, a
function cannot cross from a server component to a client component, and a page
that composed it would have to be a client component itself.

### Best practices

- The page heading is the about band's own section heading, at the page's
  `headingLevel`. There is one `h1` on the screen and the page does not have to
  police that.
- The bands after the first are untitled, so every titled thing on the page is one
  step below the page heading. That is deliberate: a section title per band is a
  prop per band whose value is a heading the page would then own.
- A person or an engagement with an `href` and no `hrefLabel` throws. A link
  announced by its address is punctuation, not a name.
- Compose at one level deeper when the page is embedded under a heading the
  consumer already owns. Pass `headingLevel="h2"` and the whole outline follows.

### Content guidelines

- The statement is present tense and about the reader's situation now. Past tense
  belongs in the milestones.
- A principle is a short name and one sentence saying what holding to it means.
- A milestone body is what changed for the reader, not what the company decided.
- The closing band asks for one thing. Two destinations is a page the consumer
  composes.
