## Overview

AuroraWash is light moving through a ground: two or three large translucent
planes in the pack's own colours, each on its own long orbit of the aurora
cycle. It follows the wash law, so the stops are semantic, the element is
`aria-hidden`, and it never touches text.

## Usage

```tsx
import { AuroraWash } from '@nanisoft/prism-ui/components/aurora-wash'

<div className="relative overflow-hidden">
  <AuroraWash className="absolute inset-0" />
  <div className="relative">{children}</div>
</div>
```

## Guidelines

### When to use

Use it where a hero's ground would otherwise be flat: a band whose claim is
that a surface is alive reads differently with light crossing it, and the wash
says that without drawing a single invented mark. It repaints with the pack,
because the planes carry the pack's own primary and series colours, so a wash
in one pack is a different wash in another.

Give it a clipped container. The wrapper clips, but the caller gives it the
positioned ancestor to fill, and a wash that escapes its band is a page-wide
stain.

### When not to use

Do not put type on it without its own contrast. A wash is a ground, and the
content over it owns the contrast the way content over `SignalField` does.

Do not use two on one page. Light moving through two grounds is two weathers,
and the reader cannot be in both.

Do not reach for it when the claim is a mechanism rather than an atmosphere. A
marker crossing a rail is `Backdrop01` with the travel cycle; the wash says a
surface is lit, not that a system runs.

### Best practices

- Let it be quiet. The intensity of a wash is the caller's, through opacity on
  the caller's own wrapper, and the restrained version is the one that reads
  as a place rather than as a poster.
- Pair it with `ParticleField` only when the two claims belong together, and
  let the tempos differ, which the aurora and drift cycles already do.
- Trust the still frame. The reduced-motion rule holds the planes at rest and
  the wash survives it, which is the test every ambient figure in this system
  passes.
