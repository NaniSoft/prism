## Overview

A Popover is a floating surface opened by a trigger. It ships as `Popover`,
`PopoverTrigger` and `PopoverContent`, all from the same module. It is
positioned by the library, flips near an edge, closes on Escape or an outside
press, and returns focus to the trigger when it closes.

A Popover is richer than a Tooltip and lighter than a Dialog. It can hold a
small form, a filter set, or a compact detail view without covering the page the
way a modal does.

## Usage

```tsx
import {
  Popover,
  PopoverContent,
  PopoverTrigger,
} from '@nanisoft/prism-ui/components/popover'

<Popover>
  <PopoverTrigger>Share</PopoverTrigger>
  <PopoverContent>
    <p className="text-sm font-medium">Share this view</p>
  </PopoverContent>
</Popover>
```

## Guidelines

### When to use

Use a Popover for content that is more than a label but not a whole screen: a
share form, a filter panel, a date range, a small preview. It keeps the reader
in context while offering more than the page shows.

### When not to use

Do not use a Popover for a required decision. An outside press can close it, so
a flow that must be completed needs a Dialog.

Do not put a single short explanation in a Popover. That is a Tooltip, which
needs no click.

Do not use a Popover as a menu of commands. A DropdownMenu carries the menu
semantics and the keyboard model a reader expects for actions.

### Best practices

- Use `side` and `align` to keep the panel near its trigger and clear of the
  content it annotates, and let the library flip it near an edge.
- Keep one task per Popover. If it needs its own tabs or steps, it has outgrown
  the surface.
- Use the trigger's `openOnHover` only when hovering is a convenience on top of
  a click, never as the only way to open.
- Because an outside press closes the popover, save or apply changes as they
  happen, or ask before discarding.
- Keep the trigger labelled. The popover's content does not name the trigger for
  assistive technology.

### Content guidelines

- Lead with a short heading that names the panel, such as "Share this view".
- Keep body copy to one or two sentences, and put detail in the form itself.
- Use sentence case for the heading and no closing period on a fragment.
