## Overview

Offer01 is a promotion band: an eyebrow, a headline, a supporting line, a code, one
action, and a closing note stating the terms.

The terms prop is required, and that is the whole argument. A discount with no
expiry, no scope and no exclusions is legally and ethically a different offer from
one with them, and a design system that lets the terms prop be optional is a design
system that has made that call for its consumers. So the only way to render this
Block without saying what an offer excludes is not to render it.

A `tone` of `primary` states its own fill and its own ink. That is the rule
`check-variant-ink` and `toast.tsx` both state, and it is here because the gate
reads the Component layer only. The measurement that made the rule is in
`Cta01Action.variant`: `Cta01` defaulted its second action to `outline` on a filled
`bg-primary` band and shipped a control that measured 1.01:1 in lavender dark.

The headline sits above the band rather than inside it, because `SectionHeading`
states its own muted ink for the eyebrow and the description, which is correct on
the page ground and wrong on a filled surface.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Offer01 } from '@nanisoft/prism-ui/blocks/offer-01'

<Offer01
  eyebrow="Through March"
  title="Two months of Observe, at the annual rate"
  code="OBSERVE12"
  codeLabel="Redemption code"
  actions={<CtaLink href="/signup?code=OBSERVE12">Redeem the code</CtaLink>}
  terms="New workspaces only, on the annual billing period, one per billing entity."
/>
```

## Guidelines

### When to use

Use Offer01 where a promotion has a condition attached to it: a discount that
expires, a trial that converts, a credit for a partner. The band exists so the
terms travel with the offer rather than three paragraphs below it.

Use `tone="primary"` where the promotion is the reason the page exists, and the
default tone where it is one section among several.

### When not to use

Do not use it for a permanent price cut. A discount that never expires is a price,
and a price belongs in `RateCard01` or `Pricing01`.

Do not use it for a newsletter signup. That is a form with an optional incentive,
and the terms are the wrong shape for consent.

### Best practices

- Pass a `codeLabel` whenever you pass a `code`. The chip announces as six
  characters otherwise, and a reader who has been told to enter a code has to be
  told which of the four things on the band is the code.
- Keep the code a string. A code is copied, compared and typed, and a node lets a
  caller compose one out of fragments a release before it ships with a space in it.
- Put the control in `actions` as a slot, not as a label and a destination. A
  router link, a form and a menu are all controls, and this Block cannot render
  three of those four if it declares its own `href`.
- Read the `secondary` and `outline` weights on a `primary` band, never `default`.
  See `Cta01Action.variant` for the measurement.

### Content guidelines

- Write the headline as what the reader gets, not as a percentage. "Two months of
  Observe, at the annual rate" survives being read on its own; "Save 17 percent"
  does not.
- Write the terms as conditions a reader could check: the expiry, the scope, the
  exclusions, and what happens to an existing customer. A sentence about the
  promotion's generosity is not a term.
- Write the code in the case you want it copied in. Readers retype these.
