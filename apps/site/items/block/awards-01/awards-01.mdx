## Overview

Awards01 is a list of recognitions: who gave it, what for, and the year it was
given, as hairline rows or as cards.

This is a separate Item from `logo-cloud-01` because a logo and an award are not
the same kind of claim. A logo is a mark, and a mark says only who works with us,
which is a fact about a relationship and one that does not expire. An award is a
sentence: a body decided something about a piece of work, on a date, and wrote it
down. The year is the part of the sentence that expires and the part a reader
checks, so it is a required field here and it is set in the mono face beside the
organisation rather than inside the body copy.

A missing year throws rather than rendering a gap. An undated award cannot be
stale, so it never goes out of date and never has to be withdrawn, which is what
makes it a logo with words attached.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Awards01 } from '@nanisoft/prism-ui/blocks/awards-01'

<Awards01
  title="Recognition"
  layout="list"
  awards={[
    {
      id: 'ardent-2025',
      body: 'Settlement Reconciliation Platform',
      organisation: 'Ardent Clearing Awards',
      year: '2025',
      category: 'Infrastructure',
      href: 'https://awards.example/2025',
    },
  ]}
/>
```

## Guidelines

### When to use

Use Awards01 when the recognitions are dated records with an awarding body behind
them, which is what makes them checkable. Between one and eight rows reads well;
past a dozen a list of recognitions is a page of its own.

Use `layout="cards"` when the work has a name long enough to want a heading of its
own, and the default list arrangement when the recognitions share one shape and
the year is the thing a reader is checking.

### When not to use

Do not use it for a band of marks. A logo says who works with us and needs no
date, so it belongs in `logo-cloud-01`.

Do not use it for a general assurance. "Recognised by leading industry bodies" is
an undated claim, and it is what `trust-strip-01` is for. Swapping a trust strip
for this Block is an upgrade from an assurance to a record, which is an editorial
and legal decision.

Do not use it for a certification or a standard. That is `compliance-01`, which
carries a review date and an evidence link rather than a year and an awarding
body.

### Best practices

- Never fill a year in yourself. If the awarding body has not published a date,
  the recognition is not one you can publish, and this Block throws rather than
  guess.
- Write the work name as the awarding body spells it. The name is read aloud as
  the row's content and a different spelling reads as a correction of their
  record.
- Pass `href` only where the public record is a URL you have checked. A row with
  no `href` is read rather than followed, which is correct.
- Order the rows yourself. Most readers want recency, but what a consumer is
  proudest of is its decision.

### Content guidelines

- Keep the organisation to its own name for itself. A body names its awards; the
  consumer does not rename them.
- Use the body's own category words where they exist. A category is a label a
  reader scans past, and a consumer's invented vocabulary makes the label a
  second claim.
