## Overview

Testimonial01 is one quote with an attribution, or two side by side.

The attribution is the load-bearing part, and everything else on the Block is
arranged around it. A quote with no name is a sentence the reader cannot weigh: an
unattributed sentence on a marketing page is a claim the page makes about itself,
wearing somebody's voice. A quote with a name and no role is only slightly better,
because a name is a name.

So `role` and `company` are two optional fields rather than one `byline` string. A
testimonial whose speaker has a role and no company is ordinary, and one byline
string would have forced that speaker to put something in the company half.

`companyUrl` renders a real anchor with the company as its accessible name, and
`avatar.name` is the caller's own fallback text rather than initials this Block
derived from a name.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Testimonial01 } from '@nanisoft/prism-ui/blocks/testimonial-01'

<Testimonial01
  title="Two operators, two different reasons"
  quotes={[
    {
      id: 'capture',
      quote: 'The replay is the part that sold the team.',
      name: 'Ines Ravel',
      role: 'Head of Market Data',
      company: 'Ardent Clearing',
      companyUrl: 'https://example.com/ardent-clearing',
    },
  ]}
/>
```

## Guidelines

### When to use

Use Testimonial01 where a reader is deciding whether to believe the page, and a
sentence from somebody with a name is the evidence.

Use `format="pair"` where two readers' situations differ, so one quote does not
speak for a team it is not on. Two is the limit: a third column is a list, and a
list of quotes is a testimonials page.

### When not to use

Do not use it for a logo wall. A logo says a company bought something; a quote
says what a person noticed, and the two are not interchangeable.

Do not use it for a rating. A five-star scale is a consumer idiom and this package
already answers it with `meter`.

### Best practices

- Give every quote a `name`. It is the one field with no default, because a quote
  with no name is a sentence the reader cannot weigh.
- Use `role` and `company` independently. A consultant, a regulator and an
  engineer who has left all have a role and no company, and an empty field is the
  honest answer where "Independent" is a claim.
- Pass `companyUrl` where the company has a page of its own. A bare URL under a
  caption is a link a reader has to read character by character.
- Pass `avatar.name` as the initials or monogram you want. This Block does not
  derive them, because taking initials from a name is a claim about the grammar of
  names in a language this repository has no data for.
- Match the quote count to the format. `single` takes one and `pair` takes two, and
  a mismatch is a thrown diagnostic rather than a short row.

### Content guidelines

- Write the quote in the person's words, not the product's. A testimonial that
  reads like a spec sheet is a spec sheet with a name on it.
- Write one or two sentences. A quote that runs past four lines is an article, and
  an article in a card is a card nobody finishes.
- Do not start every quote with the product's name. Three quotes that all open
  with it are one quote in three costumes.
