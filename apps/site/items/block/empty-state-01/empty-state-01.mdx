## Overview

EmptyState01 is the state a region shows when it has nothing in it: a dashed
frame, a mark, a headline, a sentence and at most one action. Every string is
passed in as a prop.

**The design decision is the required `reason` prop, and it is why this is a Block
rather than a Component.** An empty state looks like one thing, which is why it
gets written by hand in every product that has one, and it is actually three
things that want different words and different actions:

| Reason | What is true | The action is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `first-run` | nothing has ever existed here | make the first one |
| `no-match` | things exist and the reader's own filter removed all of them | widen or clear the filter |
| `not-permitted` | things exist and this reader may not see them | ask for access, or change context |

A component with a `title` and a `body` and an `action` cannot express that
difference, so every consumer re-decides it in their own words and at least one
of them gets it wrong: a permission boundary offering "Create your first
project", or a first-run state that says "No results found". Naming the reason
forces the decision into the open, and gives the Block something to enforce.

The set is closed at three and a fourth such as `error` or `loading` is
deliberately absent. Those are states the surrounding surface already owns: a
region that is erroring has an `Alert` and a region that is loading has a
`Spinner`. A Block that drew them would give a consumer two places to put the
same fact.

## Usage

```tsx
import { EmptyState01 } from '@nanisoft/prism-ui/blocks/empty-state-01'

// Nothing has ever existed here, so the action creates.
<EmptyState01
  reason="first-run"
  title="No invoices yet"
  body="An invoice is what you send when you need to be paid."
  actionLabel="Create your first invoice"
  onAction={openComposer}
/>

// Things exist and the reader's filter removed all of them, so the action
// clears rather than creates.
<EmptyState01
  reason="no-match"
  title="No invoices match"
  body="Three filters are active. Clearing them brings back everything you can see."
  actionLabel="Clear the filters"
  onAction={resetFilters}
/>

// A permission boundary has no next step, so it gets no button at all.
<EmptyState01
  reason="not-permitted"
  title="Billing is not on your plan"
  body="Ask an owner of this workspace to enable it."
/>
```

## Guidelines

### When to use

Use it for a region the reader has looked at and found empty. The region has to
be one the reader expected to have content: an empty region that was always going
to be empty is not a state, it is the absence of a feature.

Use `first-run` the first time a reader arrives at a region they have never used.
It is the one reason where the action creates.

Use `no-match` when the reader's own filter or search removed everything. It is
the most common of the three and the one most often written as `first-run` by
mistake, which is how a reader who has four thousand invoices is told they have
none.

Use `not-permitted` when the content exists and this reader may not see it. It is
the reason that must not offer a create action, and the reason the frame is not
tinted: a permission boundary is not a failure.

### When not to use

Do not use it for an error. A region that failed has an `Alert` and a `Spinner` or
a retry, and an empty state next to a failed request tells the reader the request
succeeded with nothing in it.

Do not use it for loading. That is a `Spinner` beside a region of the right size,
or `Skeleton`s if the shape is known. An empty state is a resting state.

Do not use it for a first run you will immediately follow with onboarding that
replaces the region. If the reader is going to be walked through creating the
first thing, that flow is the surface and the empty state is a frame it renders
inside.

Do not use it inside a control, a row or a cell. This is a region. A table with
no rows is not a table with an empty state in it.

### Best practices

- Say which of the three it is, every time. The cost of naming it is one word and
  the cost of guessing is a reader invited to create a thing that already exists.
- Give a permission boundary no action if the reader has no route. A button the
  reader cannot use is worse than no button, and inventing one is the failure
  this Block is shaped to prevent.
- Keep the headline to what is not here and the body to what to do about it. A
  headline that explains and a body that repeats gives a reader scanning the
  headline nothing to scan.
- Pass the mark as a slot rather than accepting Prism's. The shape that means
  "nothing here yet" and the shape that means "you cannot see this" are different
  shapes, and a Block that picked one would be wrong for two of its three reasons.
- Put everything else in the surrounding surface. One action is the next step;
  three actions is a form in a region that has nothing to submit.
- Do not let a heading go here. The empty state is replaced by content that will
  be a sibling of whatever introduced it, and an outline entry that disappears
  when data arrives is an entry a reader navigating by heading cannot follow.

### Content guidelines

- Write the headline as a fact about the region, not as a message to the reader:
  "No invoices match" beats "Nothing to see here". The region is empty; the
  reader is fine.
- Write the body as the next step in words, for the reader who never finds the
  button. "Three filters are active" is a sentence a reader can act on without
  the button being in their view yet.
- Name the reason's action for what it does: "Clear the filters", not "Reset".
  "Create your first invoice" and "Create an invoice" are different buttons and
  only one of them is true on a first run.
- Do not apologise in an empty state. "Oops, something went wrong" describes a
  failure, and the region is not in a failure.
