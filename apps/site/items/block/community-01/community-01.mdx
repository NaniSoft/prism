## Overview

Community01 is where a product's community is: a set of channels or spaces, each
with a name, a description, a member or activity reading, and a way in. It is a
grid of cards or a list of rows, and both forms are one set of data.

The upstream pattern this is a reading of is a community section, and the
translation is worth stating first. A pipeline that runs has an on-call rotation,
a market that is captured has a watchlist, and an estate that is observed has an
operators channel. Those are the same object: a named place, a sentence about what
belongs in it, a reading of how many people are in it, and a link. What the
upstream pattern adds is a wall of member faces, and a wall of faces is a claim
about who a product's users are, drawn from an avatar a consumer uploaded, in a
component that has no way to know whether those faces consented to being on a
marketing page. So the faces are not here, and `description` is a slot for a
consumer who wants them.

`members` and `online` are numbers, and `membersLabel` and `onlineLabel` are
functions. That is the whole decision: the counts are measurements and the words
are sentences, the Block cannot know which sentence a product's readers want, and
a bare numeral beside a channel name tells a screen reader nothing about what it
counts.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Community01 } from '@nanisoft/prism-ui/blocks/community-01'
import { Bell, LifeBuoy, Radio } from 'lucide-react'

<Community01
  eyebrow="Where to find us"
  title="Three places, and they are not the same"
  description="Names, readings and the words on every link are yours. The Block supplies the frame."
  variant="cards"
  spaces={[
    {
      id: 'on-call',
      name: 'The on-call rotation',
      description: 'A page when a run stops, and a thread afterwards about why it did.',
      icon: Bell,
      members: 1204,
      membersLabel: (count) =>
        new Intl.NumberFormat().format(count) + ' people carry the rota',
      online: 12,
      onlineLabel: (count) => `${count} on call right now`,
      href: 'https://status.example.com/rota',
      hrefLabel: 'See who is on call',
    },
    {
      id: 'watchlist',
      name: 'The watchlist',
      description: 'The instruments we publish about, and the notes we keep beside them.',
      icon: Radio,
      members: 640,
      membersLabel: (count) => new Intl.NumberFormat().format(count) + ' subscribers',
      href: 'https://example.com/watchlist',
      hrefLabel: 'Read the watchlist',
    },
    {
      id: 'operators',
      name: 'The operators channel',
      description: 'For the eleven sites, and for the people who are awake at three in the morning.',
      icon: LifeBuoy,
      href: 'https://example.com/operators',
      hrefLabel: 'Ask a question',
    },
  ]}
/>
```

## Guidelines

### When to use

Use it for the places a reader can go to talk to other readers or to the people
who build the thing. It reads well at three to eight spaces.

Use `variant="rows"` for a long list or a narrow column, where the two readings
and the link want to be on one line.

### When not to use

Do not use it for your own team. An estate's staff and a community's members are
different sets with different reasons to be listed, and `team-01` is the shape for
the first.

Do not use it for a forum index the platform already draws well. If the platform
can list the channels with live counts, this Block is a fallback rather than an
improvement.

### Best practices

- Pass both label functions wherever you pass a count. The Block throws without
  them, because a bare numeral beside a channel name tells a screen reader nothing
  about what it counts.
- Format the count inside your own label function. `Intl.NumberFormat` is the
  honest route and the Block does not own your locale.
- Write a different `hrefLabel` per space. Joining, reading, asking and
  introducing yourself are four different offers.
- Use `description` as a slot. It takes a sentence, a sentence and a link, or a
  sentence with a count in it, and all three are real.
- Use `icon` for a Lucide mark only. A real avatar is application content, and a
  set of licensed marks held in a design system is a set of marks that will be
  wrong within a year.

### Content guidelines

- `name` is the space's own name, as the space spells it.
- `description` says what belongs in the space, in one or two sentences.
- `membersLabel` is the whole reading, unit and all. Write the sentence your
  readers would say rather than a count with a noun beside it.
- `onlineLabel` says it is a moment. A reader who cannot tell a total from a
  moment has been told nothing.
