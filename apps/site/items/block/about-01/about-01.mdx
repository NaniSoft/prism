## Overview

About01 is the band that says what a thing is and who it is for. It opens with a
section title, then a large statement in the present tense, a paragraph under it
at the reading measure, a grid of principles as cards, and an optional row of
figures composed onto `Metric`.

The whole of its content is props. The statement, the paragraph, the principles,
the figures and the actions are all passed in, and the Block ships none of them,
because an about band that hardcoded its statement would install a paragraph about
somebody else's product into every consumer's page.

The decision that shapes it is the tense. An about band is present tense and is
about the product and the reader. A story is past tense and is about a sequence,
and `Story01` is the Block for that. A section that blurs the two produces a page
whose middle is decorative, because the one thing a reader looks for in the middle
of a marketing page is what the product does, and a paragraph about last year is
not an answer to it.

## Usage

```tsx
import { About01 } from '@nanisoft/prism-ui/blocks/about-01'

<About01
  eyebrow="What this is"
  title="Observation for industrial systems"
  statement="A pipeline that watches what a plant does, decides what changed, and sends an agent to deal with it."
  body={<>Two sentences of supporting copy, with a <a href="/why">link</a> in it.</>}
  figures={[
    { label: 'Sites observed', value: '1,240' },
    { label: 'Median time to a decision', value: '18 min', delta: -0.24, hint: 'Against the same period last year' },
  ]}
  principles={[
    { id: 'instrumented', title: 'Show the mechanism', body: 'The figure a reader stops on is the one that would still be true with the motion stopped.' },
    { id: 'own-work', title: 'Agents do the work', body: 'A finding that nobody acts on is a dashboard, and a dashboard is not a system.' },
  ]}
  actions={[
    { label: 'Read the architecture', href: '/architecture' },
    { label: 'Talk to us', href: '/contact' },
  ]}
/>
```

## Guidelines

### When to use

Use About01 as the section that answers "what is this and is it for me". It reads
best with three or four principles and with figures only when the band has figures
that mean something on their own.

Use it once per page. A second about band is a second claim about the same thing.

### When not to use

Do not use it for a history. Past tense and a sequence of dates is `Story01`.

Do not use it for a benefits grid. Every row of a benefits grid is a promise about
what the reader gets, and this band says what the product is and how it is built.
A page that has both should use both.

Do not use it for a list of features. `FeatureGrid01` is that, and it is a shorter
claim than a statement.

### Best practices

- Write the statement so a reader could repeat it to somebody else. If it needs
  two sentences, it belongs in `body`.
- Set `layout="statement-centre"` only when the principles are evidence rather
  than the subject. The section heading stays left aligned either way, because a
  centred title above a left-aligned grid reads as two unrelated pieces.
- Pass a `hint` on a figure whose period or source matters. A figure with no hint
  is a number a reader has to decide how much to trust.
- Keep each principle body to about twenty words. Longer belongs in the link the
  action row points at.

### Content guidelines

- The statement is one sentence in the present tense about the product and the
  reader.
- A principle title is two or three words in sentence case and names a commitment
  rather than a feature.
- A figure label says what is measured, in the reader's words, not the system's.
