## Overview

Careers01 lists open roles. Each row carries the role, the team, the location, the
working pattern, a sentence of summary where there is room, and a link to apply.
The rows are a list by default and cards on request, and there is a slot for the
caller's own team and location controls.

A role that has closed is rendered dimmed with the caller's own `closedLabel`
beside it, and the Block throws when a closed role arrives without that label. A
reader who was told a role existed and finds it gone learns nothing, and a list
that silently shrinks is a list a reader stops trusting.

The framing behind this is the recruiting pattern translated. A pipeline that runs
needs people watching it, and the shape every one of these marketing sites wants is
the same: here is the work, here is who does it, here is where they sit, and here
is the sentence that says what applying does. A Block that shipped the words would
put a headcount into every consumer's careers page, so the roles, the teams, the
locations and the closed label are all props.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Careers01 } from '@nanisoft/prism-ui/blocks/careers-01'

<Careers01
  eyebrow="Working here"
  title="Somebody has to watch the pipeline"
  description="The plant runs whether or not anybody is looking at it, and that is the job."
  closedLabel="No longer open"
  roles={[
    {
      id: 'controls',
      title: 'Controls engineer',
      team: 'Instrumentation',
      location: 'Bristol',
      pattern: 'Hybrid',
      summary: 'The layer between a sensor and an agent that has to be able to act on it.',
      href: '/careers/controls-engineer',
      hrefLabel: 'Apply for this role',
    },
    {
      id: 'agent-runtime',
      title: 'Agent runtime engineer',
      team: 'Platform',
      location: 'Remote, UK and EU',
      href: '/careers/agent-runtime',
      hrefLabel: 'Apply for this role',
    },
  ]}
/>
```

## Guidelines

### When to use

Use Careers01 on a page whose subject is working at the company, and on a page
where the roles are the content. It reads best with between one and twelve rows.

Use `variant="cards"` when the page has narrowed to two or three roles and each one
deserves a summary.

### When not to use

Do not use it for a team directory. A directory answers who works here; this
answers what is open, and the difference is the whole of the section.

Do not build a `teams` prop in front of it. The `filters` slot is where the
caller's own control goes, and a list filtered here would be filtering a copy of
data the caller owns.

### Best practices

- Pass `closedLabel` whenever a role is closed, and write the words you mean. The
  Block throws without it, and the reason is that a dimmed row with no explanation
  is a row a reader cannot tell from a rendering fault.
- Pass `filters` as your own composed control, wire it to a query you fetch, and
  hand the narrowed list back through `roles`. Nothing in the Block needs to know.
- Pass `empty` for a filter that has narrowed the list to nothing, because only
  the caller knows whether the filter or the data is the reason.
- Compose `RelativeTime` for `postedAt` so the date reads in the reader's locale,
  or pass a string your own content model has already formatted.

### Content guidelines

- A role title is the title in the posting, in the company's own words.
- The team name is the name a reader would recognise on an org chart.
- A location is a place and, where relevant, the jurisdictions it covers.
- `closedLabel` is one short phrase in the reader's language, not a status token.
