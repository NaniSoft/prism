## Overview

NoteGrid01 is a grid of short points: a title and a sentence or two, on a hairline
above each one, with no tile and no icon.

Three of the four NaniSoft sites compose this section, immediately after the
process rail and before the next numbered band, and each one drew it as the same
thing: a multi-column set of short points, each a bold title and a short
paragraph, with no card and no icon. All three also composed it *twice* - once
under the rail and once under a different section - so three hand-written grids
became six, each with its own column count and its own spacing.

It is a `dl` rather than a grid of `div`s, because each note is a term and its
explanation and a definition list is what says so.

## Usage

```tsx
import { NoteGrid01 } from '@nanisoft/prism-ui/blocks/note-grid-01'

<NoteGrid01
  title="What it captures"
  notes={[
    { title: 'One capture, one minute', body: 'The whole surface, every market minute.' },
    { title: 'One canonical timestamp', body: 'Every row carries the same clock.' },
  ]}
/>
```

## Guidelines

### When to use

Use NoteGrid01 where a section needs to make several points that each need a
sentence, and the points are peers.

Use it where a card would be too tight for the words. That is the whole
difference from `FeatureGrid01`: a card carries a title and a fragment, and a
note carries a title and a paragraph.

### When not to use

Do not use it for features. `FeatureGrid01` is for that, with its icon tiles. A
note with an icon in front of it reads as a feature and is read as one, and a
section of twelve features is a page that argues rather than explains.

Do not use it where one of the points is much longer than the others. The grid is
scanned by row, and one long body makes its own row the only one read.

### Best practices

- Keep a body to three lines at the widest viewport. Past that the detail belongs
  in the section's `description`, where it applies to the set.
- Keep every title to one line. The grid is scanned down its left edge, and a
  title that wraps costs every other row its alignment.
- Four notes is the comfortable case. Six is the limit at the largest viewport.
- Use `caption` for the sentence that qualifies the set. It is the honest place
  for "and here is what it does not do".

### Content guidelines

- Write a title as a short noun phrase, not a sentence.
- Write a body as one or two sentences that stand alone. A reader may read one row
  and stop.
- Do not start every title with the same word. Six titles that all begin "One" are
  a list of the same claim.
