## Overview

NothingChosen01 is the state of a region that can hold a record and has none
chosen in it yet: the caller's words, an optional sentence under them, and an
optional mark of the caller's choosing. It draws no action, no default sentence,
no frame and no height floor.

**It is a statement about a reader's pointer, and that is why it is a Block beside
EmptyState01 rather than a reason on it.** The four reasons EmptyState01
takes are answers to one question: what happened to the collection this region
would have drawn? Nothing has ever existed in it, the reader's own narrowing
emptied it, the reader's own earlier action moved every record out of it, or this
reader may not see it. Each is a statement about a set, and the frame
those four share is the assertion that follows from them together: there is
nothing here to read.

Nothing chosen is not that. No collection has been emptied, narrowed or hidden,
the records are in the index a few centimetres to the left, and what is missing is
the reader's pointer at one of them. A reason here would have to keep that frame
honest by claiming this region has nothing to read while a readable region sits
next to it, which is the failure a separate Block exists rather than to commit.

The newest of those four shows the line most sharply, because a region the reader
emptied has the same frame and the opposite neighbourhood. A bin a reader has just
cleared has no readable neighbour, so the shared frame is honest there and the
reason belongs on EmptyState01 beside the other three. This pane has a full index
beside it, so the same frame would be a claim the reader can watch fail.

| | What is true | What the frame claims |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `first-run` | nothing has ever existed in the collection | there is nothing here to read |
| `no-match` | things exist and the reader's own narrowing removed them | there is nothing here to read |
| `emptied-by-reader` | things existed and this reader's own action moved them out | there is nothing here to read |
| `not-permitted` | things exist and this reader may not see them | there is nothing here to read |
| `NothingChosen01` | nothing is chosen, and the index beside it is full | nothing, because it draws no frame |

**A union with one member the caller must still name is a constant with a type,
and that is the shortest statement of the difference.** The `reason` prop exists so
a caller chooses between causes that want different words and different actions.
Here there is one cause, it is not a choice, and every caller would pass the same
member, so the arm would encode a fact rather than describe a decision. This Block
therefore declares no reason, and a reader can tell by looking at the catalogue
that a fourth empty state exists without reading a union member.

## Usage

```tsx
import { NothingChosen01 } from '@nanisoft/prism-ui/blocks/nothing-chosen-01'

// The resting pane of a split, beside an index that is full.
<div className="grid gap-4 md:grid-cols-[minmax(0,3fr)_minmax(0,2fr)]">
  <InvoiceIndex rows={invoices} onSelectedIdsChange={setSelected} />
  {selected === null ? (
    <NothingChosen01
      title="Select an invoice"
      body="Choose one from the list to see its lines, its history and who it is for."
    />
  ) : (
    <InvoiceDetail invoice={selected} />
  )}
</div>

// The mark is a slot, so the shape is the product's own and it is never read aloud.
<NothingChosen01 title="No customer chosen" icon={<UserRound />} />
```

## Guidelines

### When to use

Use it for the pane that shows the record a reader has chosen from the index beside
it, before they have chosen one. The region has to be one that is capable of
holding a record, or the state does not exist.

Use it wherever the caller's arrangement has no separate screen for the record. The
narrow arrangement, where the record is its own page and the index is its own
screen, has no detail pane and therefore no empty one, which is the cheapest way
out of this state available anywhere.

### When not to use

Do not use it for an empty collection. That is EmptyState01, and the difference is
the whole point: a narrowing that matched nothing, or a bin the reader emptied
themselves, has made the region genuinely empty, and nothing chosen has not.

Do not use it for a record that cannot be rendered. A selection pointing at a
record the consumer cannot load has three causes that want three different
sentences, and every empty reason would be false except not-permitted, which is
already the caller's answer: draw EmptyState01 at that reason, or an Alert, in the
caller's own words.

Do not use it for a region with nothing in it on the reader's first arrival. A
detail pane that was never capable of holding anything is not a state a reader is
in, it is the absence of a feature.

### Best practices

- Write the words as an instruction about the other pane, not about this one.
  "Select an invoice" names what is beside it and what to do; "Nothing here" is
  false, because there is a full list a few centimetres away.
- Do not offer an action in it. The next step is a click in the index, and a
  button here is either a control this Block cannot wire or a second copy of an
  affordance the index already draws for the same reader.
- Do not widen the index to make the pane go. Tracks hold their ratio at every
  selection state, so a split that resizes when a row is clicked moves the list
  out from under the hand that clicked it. What changes is content, and forty rows
  beside one sentence is not a competition.
- Pass the mark as a slot rather than accepting Prism's. The shape that means
  "choose something" is a claim about the product's records, and four products
  calling their records four different things would each be handed an icon wrong
  for the other three.
- Keep the tracks' ratio. The split does not collapse at a narrow viewport and
  which pane survives is the caller's composition, because which pane has
  something in it is selection state and a Block that holds none cannot read it.

### Content guidelines

- Write the title as what the reader does next. It is read first and it is the
  only thing guaranteed to be read.
- Use the body for what the pane will show, not for reassurance. "Nothing is wrong"
  in an empty pane is a claim about a product this Block cannot see.
- Do not apologise. Nothing is broken, nothing is missing and nobody is blocked; a
  reader who has not chosen a record yet is at the beginning of a decision, not
  looking at a failure.
- Do not let a heading go here. The pane outlives its contents, so an outline
  entry would vanish on the first click and a reader navigating by heading would
  follow it to where it no longer exists.
