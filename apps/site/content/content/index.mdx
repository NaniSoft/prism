This section is writing guidance, not design-system documentation. Five of its
pages document how NaniSoft writes product copy. The sixth, the Glossary,
documents the design system's own vocabulary.

- [About content](/content/about-content) defines what content is and who this
  section is for.
- [Audience](/content/audience) describes the people product copy serves.
- [Voice](/content/voice) fixes the voice and sets tone by context.
- [Writing mechanics](/content/writing-mechanics) covers capitalisation,
  punctuation, grammar and structure.
- [Product vocabulary](/content/product-vocabulary) lists preferred terms and
  the terms to avoid.
- [Glossary](/content/glossary) defines the design system's vocabulary.

Every rule in this section is prefixed `RULE:` so it can be found and checked by
tooling. The words MUST, SHOULD and MAY are used deliberately here, and only
here: they carry the RFC 2119 meaning. Item pages use plain language instead.
